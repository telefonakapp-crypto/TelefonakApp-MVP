# Telefonak - Product Vision

**Version:** 1.0  
**Status:** Approved (Living Document)  
**Last Updated:** July 2026

---

# 1. Executive Summary

Telefonak is Jordan's first dedicated marketplace designed specifically for buying and selling used mobile phones and smartwatches.

Unlike general marketplaces, Telefonak provides a structured, trusted, and user-friendly experience built around the unique requirements of consumer electronics.

By combining structured listings, intelligent filtering, and human moderation, Telefonak aims to become the most trusted platform in Jordan for buying and selling used mobile devices.

---

# 2. Why Telefonak Exists

Buying and selling a used phone in Jordan is unnecessarily difficult.

Today, most people rely on platforms such as Facebook Marketplace, Facebook Groups, and OpenSooq.

While these platforms allow users to post listings, they were never designed specifically for electronics.

As a result:

- Buyers waste time searching through hundreds of unrelated listings.
- Sellers struggle to reach the right audience.
- Search results are inconsistent.
- Device specifications are often missing.
- Listings vary in quality.
- Buyers cannot accurately compare similar devices.

A person looking for a black iPhone 17 Pro with 256 GB storage, 95% battery health, in Amman currently has no efficient way to find exactly that device.

Telefonak exists to solve this problem.

---

# 3. Mission

Our mission is to make buying and selling used mobile devices simple, transparent, and trustworthy.

Every decision made during development should contribute to one or more of these goals.

---

# 4. Vision

To become Jordan's most trusted marketplace for used consumer electronics and establish the standard for how electronic devices are bought and sold online.

After establishing a strong presence in Jordan, Telefonak will continue expanding its services, product categories, and regional reach.

---

# 5. The Problem

Current marketplaces suffer from several major limitations:

## Poor Search

Users must manually search through hundreds of unrelated listings.

## Limited Filtering

Users cannot accurately filter devices by:

- Model
- Storage
- Color
- Battery Health
- Condition
- Location

## Unstructured Listings

Every seller writes listings differently.

Important information is often missing.

## Low Trust

Users frequently encounter:

- Fake listings
- Incorrect specifications
- Misleading photos
- Spam

## Poor User Experience

General marketplaces prioritize every category equally.

Electronics deserve their own dedicated experience.

---

# 6. Our Solution

Telefonak provides a marketplace designed specifically for electronics.

Every listing follows a structured format instead of relying on free text.

Users choose predefined values for:

- Device Category
- Brand
- Model
- Storage
- Color
- Condition
- Battery Health
- Price
- Location

This creates:

- Better search accuracy
- Better filtering
- Better marketplace quality
- Better user experience

# 8. Core Product Principles

Every feature must support one or more of the following principles.

## Simplicity

The application should be intuitive for users of all technical backgrounds.

Completing an important task should require as few steps as possible.

---

## Trust

Marketplace quality is more important than marketplace size.

Listings should be accurate.

Photos should match the product.

Spam should be minimized.

---

## Speed

The application should feel fast.

Loading times should be minimized.

Searching should produce relevant results quickly.

---

## Consistency

Every listing should follow the same structure.

Users should never wonder where information is located.

---

## Accuracy

Whenever possible, users should select predefined values instead of manually typing information.

This reduces errors and improves search quality.

---

# 9. Competitive Advantage

Telefonak is not a general marketplace.

It is a specialized marketplace built exclusively for consumer electronics.

This specialization allows Telefonak to offer:

- Structured listings
- Dynamic filters
- Model-specific colors
- Model-specific storage capacities
- Battery health filtering
- Human moderation
- Better search results
- Faster buying decisions

---

# 10. Marketplace Quality

Every listing submitted by a seller enters a moderation process.

Listing Statuses:

- Pending Review
- Approved
- Rejected
- Sold
- Hidden
- Expired

Administrators review every listing before it becomes publicly visible.

The moderation process helps ensure:

- Correct photos
- Accurate descriptions
- Marketplace quality
- Reduced scams

# 14. Product Philosophy

Every new feature should answer one simple question:

**Does this make buying or selling a phone easier, faster, or more trustworthy?**

If the answer is no, the feature should be reconsidered.
